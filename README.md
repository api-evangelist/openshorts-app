OpenShorts provides a free AI-powered clip maker and clipping tool, enabling users to create short videos from longer content across platforms like YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Twitch. The service is open source, offering self‑hosting options and a range of automation features such as auto‑reframe, auto‑clip, and AI‑generated thumbnails. It supports various use cases including podcasts, sermons, gaming clips, and social media content, with both free and paid plans. OpenShorts emphasizes privacy, not storing personal data beyond necessary operational logs, and does not use user content to train AI models.

**Areas:** Video · AI · OpenSource · ClipMaker · Free

## How this profile is made
